Soko la Kidijitali (Digital Marketplace)

Jukwaa la wazi linalowawezesha wakulima, wafugaji, na wavuvi kutangaza mazao yao mtandaoni bure. Wanunuzi wanaweza kuona kiwango kilichopo, bei kwa kilo, na picha za mazao papo hapo.
